# Personal writing review

Each Personal assignment has an editable draft with automatic saving, a structured writing review, and an optional follow-up question thread. The review reports up to six specific suggestions in grammar, punctuation, spelling, clarity, word choice, or academic tone. Each suggestion includes the exact excerpt, a possible replacement, and a short explanation. Learners can locate the excerpt in the editor or apply a replacement themselves.

The editor does not automatically change writing. Applying a suggestion affects only its exact excerpt and is available only when that excerpt appears once in the current draft. The learner remains responsible for reviewing changes and submitting their own work.

Drafts are stored under the owning Personal assignment. Editing a draft saves it but does not make an AI request. Selecting Review draft sends the draft and assignment title/notes to the configured DeepSeek service. The last review is cached by a hash of the draft, so rerunning a review without changing the text does not consume another AI request. A changed draft requires a new review. The assignment owner check is enforced server-side.

Draft review is limited to 6,000 characters and requests at most six issue cards with a short response budget. It does not rewrite an essay or claim to be a complete grammar checker. The follow-up question feature remains available separately.
